What the Friends Do

Your membership helps the Friends advocate for and support The Colony Public Library as a cultural and educational asset for The Colony and Denton County.

  • Technology Projects

    3D printing and technology grants to support the library.

  • Book Donations

    Accepting and facilitating book donations and adoptions, including books adopted in memory or in honor of someone special.

  • Genealogy Projects

    Helping research the history of The Colony and its residents.

  • Little Free Libraries

    Working with Little Free Library to promote reading and community outreach.
