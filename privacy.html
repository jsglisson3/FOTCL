Privacy

A plain-language summary of what this website does with your information.

Draft for the Friends board to review and approve.

What we collect

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it doesn't ask you to create an account. That's why you won't see a cookie banner.

Services we link to or show

  • Membership and donations are handled by Donorbox on its own website, under its own privacy policy.
  • Videos are shown from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, which doesn't store viewing information until you play a video.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives basic technical information such as your browser type.
  • Newsletters open in Google Drive.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply. We don't sell or share them.
