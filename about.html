About the Friends

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting, enhancing and promoting the work of The Colony Public Library.

Our Mission

To advocate for and support The Colony Public Library as a cultural and educational asset to the residents of The Colony and Denton County, through fundraising and by promoting volunteer activities.

How Your Support Helps

  1. You Become a Friend

    Your membership or donation supports the Friends.

  2. The Friends Get to Work

    We advocate for and support the library through fundraising and by promoting volunteer activities.

  3. The Library Does More

    Technology projects, book donations, genealogy research and Little Free Libraries for The Colony.

Rooted in the City by the Lake

The Colony sits on the eastern shore of Lake Lewisville, with about 23 miles of shoreline. That's why it's known as the City by the Lake.

Helping research the history of The Colony and its residents is part of what the Friends do. Here are a few moments from that history.

  1. 1841

    The Peters Colony settlement contract begins under the Republic of Texas. The city later takes its name from it.

  2. 1954

    The Lake Lewisville dam, built by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, begins collecting water.

  3. 1977

    Residents vote to incorporate The Colony as a city.

  4. 1982

    The Colony Public Library opens.

  5. 2019

    The library earns the Texas Municipal Library Directors' Association Achievement of Excellence in Libraries Award. It earns it again in 2020.

  6. 2027 Coming up

    The Colony turns 50.

Sources: City of The Colony history, The Colony Public Library and Wikipedia.
