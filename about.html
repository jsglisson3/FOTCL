About the Friends

The mission of FOTCPL is to advocate for and support The Colony Public Library as a cultural and educational asset to the residents of The Colony and Denton County, through fundraising and by promoting volunteer activities.

What we do

Technology projects

3D printing and technology grants to support the library.

Book donations and memoriams

Accepting and facilitating book donations and adoptions.

Genealogy projects

Helping research the history of The Colony and its residents.

Little Free Libraries

Working with littlefreelibrary.org to promote reading and community outreach.

Business Friends

Thank you to the local businesses that support the Friends, including The Colony Chamber of Commerce.
