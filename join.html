Let's Become Friends

Joining the Friends is a great way to support our community and our local library.

Join or Renew Online See Membership Levels

Friends of The Colony Public Library is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your donation may be tax‑deductible.

Every Membership Includes

Whichever level you choose, you'll receive all of these.

  • The Quarterly Newsletter

    News from the Friends and the library, delivered to your inbox.

  • Private Event Invitations

    Invitations to events just for Friends members.

  • Member Forums

    Access to forums for Friends members.

  • 10% Off

    Save on FOTCPL books and sale items.

How to Join

  1. 1

    Choose a Level

    Pick an individual or business membership from the levels above.

  2. 2

    Join or Renew

    Pay securely online through Donorbox, or bring a check to the library.

    Join or Renew Online
  3. 3

    Enjoy Your Benefits

    The quarterly newsletter, invitations to private events, access to forums and 10% off FOTCPL books and sale items.

Our individual and business membership levels have recently been updated and if you are currently a Lifetime member, you are still entitled to all the benefits at the Friend level. You can also join by bringing a check to the library.
