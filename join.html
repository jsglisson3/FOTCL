Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question? Email us at info@fotcpl.org.

How do I join or renew?

Join or renew online with our secure Donorbox form, or bring a check to the library.

How much does a membership cost?

Individual memberships start at $15 a year, and business memberships start at $50 a year. At the Supporter level and above, you can also pay monthly.

What do members receive?

Every membership includes a subscription to the quarterly newsletter, invitations to private events, access to forums and 10% off FOTCPL books and sale items. Higher levels add annual event tickets, and some include your name in the quarterly newsletter.

Can businesses become members?

Yes. Business memberships start at $50 a year. Higher business levels add newsletter name placement and annual event tickets, and the Patron and Partner levels also include a sponsorship table and logo placement.

Is my membership tax-deductible?

Friends of The Colony Public Library is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations are tax‑deductible to the extent allowed by law. For advice about your own taxes, please check with a tax professional.

How do I get the newsletter?

A subscription to the quarterly newsletter comes with every membership. You can also read current and past editions on our Newsletters page.

Our individual and business membership levels have recently been updated and if you are currently a Lifetime member, you are still entitled to all the benefits at the Friend level. You can also join by bringing a check to the library.
